-   **Fix**: Add dummy environment variables to satisfy the entrypoint check.
    -   `DATABASES_HOST=postgres-primary`
    -   `DATABASES_PORT=5432`
    -   `DATABASES_USER=postgres`
    -   `DATABASES_PASSWORD=postgres_password`

## 2. Fix Redis Sentinel (`redis/sentinel/docker-compose.yml`)

-   **Fix**: Add a `healthcheck` to `redis-master` and make sentinels depend on it.
    -   **Redis Master Healthcheck**: `redis-cli ping || exit 1`
    -   **Sentinel Dependency**: `depends_on: redis-master: condition: service_healthy`
